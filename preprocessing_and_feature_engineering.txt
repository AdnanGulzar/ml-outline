11. fit() vs transform()
fit()

Learns parameters from data.

For example, StandardScaler learns:

mean
standard deviation
transform()

Uses those learned parameters to transform data.

fit_transform()

Equivalent conceptually to:

fit()
transform()

on the same training data.

Important rule:

X_train → fit_transform()
X_test  → transform()

Don't fit your scaler on the test set.

30. Pipelines

A Pipeline combines preprocessing and modeling.

Example:

Pipeline([
    ("scaler", StandardScaler()),
    ("pca", PCA(n_components=0.95)),
    ("model", LogisticRegression())
])

Concept:

Raw data
   ↓
Scaling
   ↓
PCA
   ↓
Model

This helps prevent data leakage and makes preprocessing reproducible.

31. Data Leakage

A critical ML concept.

Bad:

Entire dataset
      ↓
fit scaler
      ↓
train/test split

Better:

Data
 ↓
Train/Test Split
 ↓
Train → fit preprocessing
Test  → transform only

Pipeline helps enforce this correctly.
